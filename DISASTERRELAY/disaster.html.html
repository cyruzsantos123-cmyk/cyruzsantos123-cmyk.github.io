[ MISSION BRIEFING ]

DisasterRelay is a flood-rescue simulation game where players manage emergency response vehicles to help communities affected by severe flooding. Players must deliver supplies, transport injured civilians, and rescue stranded people before time runs out.

[ GAME MECHANICS ]

01. RISING FLOODWATER

Water levels increase as time passes, blocking roads and paths.

02. CIVILIAN HEALTH

Stranded civilians have three hearts. Their health decreases while waiting for rescue.

03. LEPTOSPIROSIS

Contaminated floodwater creates health risks that require hospital treatment.

04. VEHICLE LIMITS

Land vehicles slow down on flooded roads and cannot pass fully submerged paths.

05. TIME PRESSURE

The countdown adds urgency as floodwater spreads and civilian health decreases.

06. MISSION GOAL

Rescue civilians, deliver supplies, and bring patients to safety.

MISSION: SAVE THE COMMUNITY

Every second matters. Use teamwork, make smart decisions, and never leave anyone behind.
