ABOUT TETRIS

Discover the game, its purpose, and what makes it challenging.

🧩

WHAT IS TETRIS?

Tetris is a classic tile-matching puzzle video game. Different shaped blocks called tetrominoes fall from the top of the playing field. Players move and rotate them to create complete horizontal lines.

🎯

GAME OBJECTIVE

The main objective is to arrange falling blocks and complete as many horizontal lines as possible. Players earn points by clearing lines and try to keep the board from filling up.

🏆

THE CHALLENGE

Blocks fall faster as the player progresses. Good planning, quick decisions, and proper block placement help players survive longer and achieve higher scores.

⚙️

GAME MECHANICS

  • Blocks fall automatically.
  • Move blocks left or right.
  • Rotate blocks to fit spaces.
  • Complete a full row to clear it.
  • The game ends when blocks reach the top.
📈

PROGRESSION

Players gain points and clear lines to advance through levels. Each new level increases the falling speed, making the game more challenging.

🧠

SKILLS DEVELOPED

Tetris encourages spatial awareness, pattern recognition, problem-solving, decision-making, and hand-eye coordination.

HISTORY OF TETRIS

How a simple puzzle became a worldwide gaming classic.

1984 — The Beginning

Tetris was created by Alexey Pajitnov, a Soviet software engineer. The original game was inspired by puzzle games and geometric shapes.

1980s — Growing Popularity

The game spread to different computers and gaming systems. Its simple rules and addictive gameplay helped it gain popularity around the world.

Today — A Timeless Classic

Tetris continues to be played on computers, consoles, and mobile devices. It remains one of the most recognizable puzzle games in gaming history.

HOW TO PLAY

Learn the basic controls, scoring system, and strategies.

⬅️

1. MOVE THE BLOCKS

Use the left and right arrow keys or A and D to move the falling blocks into the position you want.

🔄

2. ROTATE THE BLOCKS

Press the up arrow or W key to rotate a block. This helps you fit different shapes into empty spaces.

⬇️

3. DROP THE BLOCKS

Use the down arrow for a soft drop or the spacebar for a hard drop to place the block faster.

✨

4. CLEAR THE LINES

Fill an entire horizontal row with blocks. Once complete, the row disappears and you earn points.

⏸️

5. PAUSE THE GAME

Press P or the pause button when you need a break. Press resume to continue your game.

💀

6. AVOID GAME OVER

Keep the blocks below the top of the board. The game ends when a new block can no longer enter the playing field.

SCORING SYSTEM

Earn more points by clearing multiple lines at once.

SINGLE LINE

Clear 1 line to earn 100 points × current level.

DOUBLE LINE

Clear 2 lines to earn 300 points × current level.

TRIPLE LINE

Clear 3 lines to earn 500 points × current level.

FOUR LINES

Clear 4 lines at once to earn 800 points × current level.

SOFT DROP

Earn 1 additional point for each row moved down using the soft-drop control.

HARD DROP

Earn 2 additional points when using the hard-drop control.

TIPS & STRATEGIES

Improve your gameplay with these simple techniques.

Keep the Board Organized

Try to avoid creating unnecessary gaps between blocks.

Plan Ahead

Look at the next block preview before placing your current piece.

Clear Lines Regularly

Do not let blocks stack too high. Clear rows whenever possible.

Use Rotation Wisely

Rotate pieces to fit spaces and create complete horizontal lines.

Stay Calm

As the game gets faster, focus on making smart placements.

Practice Makes Progress

Keep playing to improve your timing, accuracy, and strategy.
